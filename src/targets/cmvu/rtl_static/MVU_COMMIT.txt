Local modifications (not in MVU), 2026-09-23:
  - cmvu_mode1.sv — BIAS_WIDTH default changed 8 -> 32 (bias_in is now a 32-bit
    per-lane accumulator-scale value, sign-extended into ACC_WIDTH on acc_first,
    with the sign-extension made legal when BIAS_WIDTH == ACC_WIDTH); the
    round-half-up adder and its ACC_WIDTH+1 guard bit were removed from requant,
    which is now a plain arithmetic (floor/TRN) shift. Rounding, where wanted,
    is now supplied by the integrator via the bias.
  - docs/architecture.md and docs/mode_1_user_guide.md — requant and bias
    sections updated to match (BIAS_WIDTH=32, TRN-only requant, no
    rounding-mode/saturation ports).

cmvu_mode1.sv, docs/architecture.md and docs/mode_1_user_guide.md are therefore
no longer verbatim copies of the vendored MVU source; cmvu_w_mem.sv and
cmvu_regbank.sv are unaffected and remain verbatim.
